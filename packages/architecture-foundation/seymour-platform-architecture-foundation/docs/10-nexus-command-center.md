# Nexus Command Center

Nexus is the human-facing operations and observability layer.

## Version 1.0 responsibilities

- discovery and import
- CMDB and relationship truth
- infrastructure topology
- health and telemetry presentation
- operations execution through supported contracts
- evidence and timeline presentation
- recommendations and alerts

Nexus observes and orchestrates products; it does not silently take ownership of their private data or configuration.
